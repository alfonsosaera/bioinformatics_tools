README UPDATE GUIDE  (for an LLM adding a new tool from a LinkedIn post to README.md)

TASK
Given a LinkedIn post about a bioinformatics tool/method/resource: extract its name, one-line description,
paper/code/website links and the post URL; choose the section(s) and subsection(s) below; add the entry
to README.md following the format and rules in this file. Only use existing sections/subsections.
Never invent links: omit any field that does not exist.

======================================================================
1. SECTIONS AND SUBSECTIONS
======================================================================
(Name = exact heading text in README.md. Scope = what belongs there. "Not" = go elsewhere.)

## Single-cell RNA-seq
Tools for single-cell / single-nucleus data (mainly RNA; also scATAC, methylation, protein, scDNA, Hi-C).
Not: spatial-first tools (-> Spatial Omics); tools needing both sc and spatial data (-> Single-cell & Spatial Integration).

### Experimental Technologies
Wet-lab protocols, assays, platforms, chemistries (fixation, multiplexing kits, imaging+seq, targeted capture,
multiome/multi-omic assays, UMI design, lineage/temporal labelling) and lab-method comparisons.
### Preprocessing & Quality Control
Reads -> count matrices (pseudoalignment, UMI counting, nascent/mature, transposable-element quantification),
ambient RNA, doublets/multiplets, empty droplets, demultiplexing (HTO or genetic), imputation/denoising, off-target reads.
### Batch Integration
Batch correction and integration of datasets of the same modality (incl. cross-species, federated, reference mapping
by integration); their benchmarks.
### Dimensionality Reduction & Clustering
Embeddings, representation learning, feature selection, clustering/over-clustering, metacells, archetypes,
intrinsic dimension, de novo cell type/state discovery.
### Cell Type Annotation
Assigning labels to cells (reference/marker-based, classifiers, ontology-guided, senescence).
Not: LLM/agent annotators (-> LLMs, Agents & Chat Assistants; cross-list if also a classic method);
immune-specific annotators (-> Immune Cell Identification & Immune Repertoire).
### Differential Expression & Marker Discovery
DE testing (single-cell, pseudobulk, multi-donor, longitudinal), differential abundance, marker genes,
conditional gene-trait association.
### Trajectory Inference & Pseudotime
Pseudotime, lineage dynamics, fate probabilities, temporal forecasting, trajectory-based DE.
### RNA Velocity
Splicing-based velocity, velocity vector fields, velocity + regulation models.
### Gene Regulatory Network Inference
GRNs, TF activity/regulons, eRegulons, co-expression networks (WGCNA-like), prior-knowledge networks.
### Cell–Cell Communication
Ligand-receptor / signalling inference from scRNA-seq, differential communication, metabolite-mediated CCC, CCC benchmarks.
Not: spatially-aware CCC (-> Spatial Cell–Cell Communication).
### Functional Enrichment & Pathway Analysis
Gene-set enrichment/scoring, gene programs and signatures, pathway visualization.
### Multimodal Integration
Joint analysis of different modalities (RNA+ATAC+protein), vertical/mosaic integration, modality translation,
latent factors. Not: same-modality batch integration (-> Batch Integration).
### Simulation & Benchmarking
Simulators, synthetic/generative data models, benchmark platforms for single-cell methods.
### Visualization & Interactive Exploration
Plotting packages, viewers, portals, atlas browsers.
### Immune Cell Identification & Immune Repertoire
Immune-specific cell identification (T/B/myeloid subset and state annotation, immune reference atlases and
projection, e.g. TIL atlases), and TCR/BCR sequencing and analysis: clonotypes, antigen specificity, clonal
expansion, AIRR pipelines. Not: general-purpose annotators (-> Cell Type Annotation).
### Genotyping, CNV & Clonal/Lineage Inference
SNP/SNV/mosaic-variant calling from single-cell data, CNV/ploidy, clone inference, mtDNA, RNA editing,
expression-based lineage inference. Not: demultiplexing (-> Preprocessing & QC; cross-list if genotype-based).
### Allele-Specific Expression
Allele-specific and differential allelic expression.
### Perturbation Modeling & Virtual Cells
Perturb-seq/CRISPR-screen analysis, prediction of genetic/drug perturbation responses, virtual-cell models,
perturbation datasets and databases.
### Single-cell Proteomics
Protein-level single-cell data: CITE-seq normalization, mass-spec SCP, RNA->protein prediction, secretion.
### Single-cell Hi-C
Single-cell 3D genome / chromatin contact data.
### Long-read & Isoform-resolved Analysis
Long-read single-cell RNA, isoform quantification, differential splicing, reviews of these.
### Epigenomics & Chromatin Accessibility
scATAC-seq, DNA methylation, histone marks, enhancer/regulatory-element models, sequence-to-function models.
### Phenotype & Clinical Outcome Association
Linking cells/samples to phenotype, survival or clinical outcome.

## Spatial Omics
Spatial transcriptomics/proteomics and spatial imaging data. Not: methods requiring sc reference + spatial (-> Single-cell & Spatial Integration).

### Experimental Technologies
Wet-lab spatial assays/platforms (sequencing- or imaging-based, 3D, spatial multi-omics, subcellular spatial proteomics).
### Preprocessing & Quality Control
Transcript assignment/misassignment, unassigned RNA, large-scale pipelines, binning/rasterization, spatial smoothing.
### Cell Segmentation
Cell/nucleus segmentation for imaging and high-resolution spatial data.
### Spatial Cell Type Annotation
Cell/spot annotation in spatial data, incl. from H&E.
### Spatial Differential Expression
Niche/neighbourhood-dependent DE.
### Spatially Variable Genes, Gene Prediction & Resolution Enhancement
SVG detection, gene imputation, expression prediction from histology, super-resolution/cell-level recovery.
### Spatial Domains & Tissue Architecture
Spatial domain/niche detection, multi-slice or histology-integrated domain methods, 3D tissue structure.
### Spatial Cell–Cell Communication
Spatial ligand-receptor signalling, communication niches, spatial GRN+signalling.
### Neighborhood & Microenvironment Analysis
Cellular neighbourhoods, spatial statistics, multiplex-imaging analysis, microenvironment models.
### Spatiotemporal Dynamics
Time-series spatial data, spatial trajectories/dynamics.
### Multi-sample Spatial Integration
Slice alignment/registration, batch correction across spatial samples, multimodal spatial integration.
### Visualization & Interactive Exploration
Spatial analysis toolkits, viewers, general-purpose spatial suites.
### Simulation & Benchmarking
Spatial simulators, platform comparisons, spatial benchmarks.

## Single-cell & Spatial Integration
Methods that need BOTH single-cell and spatial (or bulk) data.

### Deconvolution
Cell-type proportion estimation in bulk or spatial spots from sc references (reference-free too).
### Spatial Cell Mapping & Reconstruction
Mapping sc cells to spatial coordinates, pseudo-space reconstruction, sc-spatial joint models.
### Benchmarking & Method Evaluation
Benchmarks/metrics for sc-spatial integration and deconvolution.

## Foundation Models & LLMs
### Single-cell Foundation Models
Pretrained large models on single-cell data, tokenization, zero-shot/embedding frameworks, cell-language models.
### Spatial Foundation Models
Pretrained models for spatial data.
### Histopathology Foundation Models
Pathology/H&E foundation models and their benchmarks.
### LLMs, Agents & Chat Assistants
LLM/agentic assistants, chat interfaces, LLM annotation and literature-mining, only when built for or applied to
single-cell/spatial data (or cell-type/marker/GRN knowledge). General biomedical LLMs are out of scope.

## Infrastructure, Resources & Applications
### Data Infrastructure & Scalable Computing
Data formats, loaders, conversion, GPU/scalable frameworks, workflows/pipelines, atlases, curated datasets,
reporting standards and guidelines, packages built for single-cell/spatial data.
### Disease Genes, Drug Response & Discovery
Drug sensitivity/response, drug-indication prediction, disease-gene prioritization, drug-perturbation databases.

======================================================================
2. DECISION RULES
======================================================================
- Modality first: single-cell -> "Single-cell RNA-seq"; spatial -> "Spatial Omics"; needs both -> "Single-cell & Spatial Integration".
- Foundation model / LLM -> "Foundation Models & LLMs"; also cross-list to a task subsection if the task is its main claim.
- Tool-vs-task: wet-lab method -> Experimental Technologies; software -> a task subsection.
- Tie-breakers: same-modality batch effects -> Batch Integration, different modalities -> Multimodal Integration;
  demultiplexing -> Preprocessing (genotype-based may also go to Genotyping); velocity-based -> RNA Velocity,
  otherwise Trajectory; benchmark of a topic -> that topic's Benchmarking/Simulation subsection of the same section.
- Works equally on single-cell and spatial data -> cross-list in the equivalent subsection of both sections.
- CROSS-LISTING: if a tool genuinely fits several subsections, add IDENTICAL entries (byte-for-byte) in every fitting one.
- OUT OF SCOPE (send to unassgined_posts2.md): bulk-only tools, non-omics tools, general genomics utilities, general
  biomedical/clinical LLMs, and variant callers not designed for single-cell data; unless explicitly tied to
  single-cell/spatial (e.g. sc-informed bulk deconvolution).

======================================================================
3. ENTRY FORMAT
======================================================================
#### ToolName
One sentence: what it does and how (no marketing, no author list). Written on the line right under the heading.
- Paper: [url](url)
- Code: [url](url)
- Website: [url](url)
- LinkedIn post: [url](url)

- Heading = official tool name with original capitalization.
- Field order: Paper, Code, Website (other labels allowed: Protocol, Tutorial, Package, R package), LinkedIn post last.
- Link text = the URL itself. Repeat a label for several links. Omit fields with no link (no empty "[]()" and no empty lines).
- Several LinkedIn posts: "- LinkedIn posts:" followed by indented "  - [url](url)" lines.
- One blank line between entries; no trailing spaces.
- Insert alphabetically (case-insensitive) inside the subsection.

======================================================================
4. WORKFLOW AND CHECKS
======================================================================
1. Duplicate check: search README.md for the tool name and its paper/DOI/repo URL.
   If it exists: do not add a new entry; add the new LinkedIn post to ALL copies (as a "LinkedIn posts:" list)
   and keep all copies identical.
2. Choose subsection(s) with sections 1-2. Add the entry/entries (section 3).
3. Editing an existing subsection needs no TOC change. The TOC (between <!-- TOC --> and <!-- /TOC -->) lists only
   "##" and "###" headings; if a subsection is added/renamed, regenerate it. GitHub anchors: lowercase, drop punctuation
   except hyphens, spaces -> hyphens (so "&" leaves a double hyphen), repeated headings get -1, -2 in document order.
4. Do not create sections/subsections and do not commit unless the user approves.
5. Report to the user: tool name, chosen subsection(s), and any missing fields.

======================================================================
5. WHEN NOTHING FITS
======================================================================
Do BOTH, and leave README.md unchanged:
a) Append to unassgined_posts2.md:
   - **ToolName** — [post url](post url) — one-line description. No fit: <reason>. Suggested: <Section> › <New subsection name> — <scope>.
b) Propose the new subsection (name + one-line scope + section) to the user and wait for approval.
   After approval: add "### Name" in the right section, add the entry, regenerate the TOC, and update this file.
Posts that are not about a specific tool (career news, events, marketing, opinion, generic education) and out-of-scope
tools (section 2) go to unassgined_posts2.md as plain lines, with no proposal: "- Post title — [url](url)".
